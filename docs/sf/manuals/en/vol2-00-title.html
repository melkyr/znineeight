Volume II — Learning Z98

Enter here if you already program in some language. Leave able to write Z98 fluently, in its own syntax and idioms.

Volume I teaches programming. This volume teaches the language. It assumes you do not need the machine explained, and it spends its pages on the parts of Z98 that differ from the languages you already know.
