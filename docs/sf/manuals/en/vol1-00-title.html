Volume I — Getting Started

Enter here if you have never written a program. Leave able to write small working programs.

This volume is the on-ramp. It is short on purpose: it exists to get you to the point where Volume II makes sense. Every chapter ends with a runnable program, a list of common mistakes, and an exercise.
